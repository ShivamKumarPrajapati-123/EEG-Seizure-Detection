Research Interpretation:
The recording-wise evaluation prevents windows from the same EEG recording from appearing in both training and testing sets.
The model was evaluated on recordings that were not used during training.
If seizure sensitivity is low despite high accuracy, the result indicates that severe class imbalance is affecting seizure detection performance.
Accuracy alone should therefore not be considered sufficient for assessing EEG seizure detection performance.
